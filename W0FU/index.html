Card. get-tuple-element is free aliasing [W0FU]

  • August 22, 2026
  • Kellen Kanarios

What is %gte.1 = f32[8]{0} get-tuple-element(%t), index=0, and why is it everywhere?

Answer It extracts one element of a tuple value. Tuples are how HLO returns more than one thing (Tuples are first-class in HLO), so every multi-output op is followed by a fan of these: while (A while loop's whole state is one tuple), a fusion with several outputs, a custom call, sort, variadic reduce (reduce removes the dims it lists). They are free at run time — pure aliasing into the tuple's buffers, no data moves. So read them as punctuation, not as work. A while body that is half get-tuple-element at the top and tuple at the bottom is not doing anything; it is unpacking and repacking the carry. MLIR avoids the whole idiom by giving ops multiple results natively (A multi-result op binds one name and indexes into it).
