Card. Gather before, or reduce after [JO4V]

  • August 12, 2026
  • Kellen Kanarios

Sharded matmul, case 2: one multiplicand has a sharded contracting dimension, \(A[I, J_X] \cdot B[J, K] \to C[I, K]\). Which primitive, and what is the alternative?

Answer AllGather the sharded input first, then matmul locally: \(\mathrm {AllGather}_X(A[I, J_X]) \to A[I, J]\), then \(A[I, J] \cdot B[J, K] \to C[I, K]\). You cannot just multiply the local shards, because the sum over \(J\) is split across \(X\) and each device holds only a partial sum. The alternative is to treat it like case 3: do the local partial matmul and AllReduce or ReduceScatter the result. That shards the FLOPs but usually costs more comms. For \(X[B,D] \cdot Y[D_X,F]\), the gather moves \(2DF\) bytes and the reduce moves \(2 \cdot 2BF\), so the reduce path wins roughly when \(D > 2B\). Rare in practice, though — the other input is usually sharded along the same axis anyway, as under FSDP.
