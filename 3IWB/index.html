Card. A sharding annotation is a tiling plus a device order [3IWB]

  • August 22, 2026
  • Kellen Kanarios

Decode sharding={replicated}, sharding={devices=[2,4]<=[8]}, sharding={devices=[2,4]<=[4,2]T(1,0)}, sharding={maximal device=0}

Answer
  • replicated — a full copy on every device.
  • devices=[2,4] — the tensor is tiled two ways on dim 0 and four ways on dim 1. The <=[8] is shorthand for the device list being iota over eight devices.
  • <=[4,2]T(1,0) — that iota device list is reshaped to [4,2] and transposed before being laid over the tiles. Same tiling, different device-to-tile assignment.
  • maximal device=0 — computed on exactly one device.
The device order is not cosmetic. It decides which mesh axis is "fast" — whether neighbouring tiles sit on neighbouring chips over ICI or across a slower hop — so two Sharding annotations with identical tilings can differ by a large factor in collective cost (replica_groups says who reduces with whom). A trailing last_tile_dim_replicate means the final tile dimension is replication rather than data.
