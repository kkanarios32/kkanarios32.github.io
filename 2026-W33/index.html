3. Goals for Next Week

  • August 14, 2026
  • Kellen Kanarios

I am but a humble nomad in my long journey toward TPU sensei-ship. Unfortunately (or fortunately?), I am still firmly on the left end of the Dunning-Kruger curve. I am progressing slowly but very dilligently through the How to scale your model. I want to be on chapter 5 by end of next week. The remaining chapter 3 exercises seem like they may take awhile and chapter 4 is equally important as it is dense, so I want to make sure I give it a proper treatment. An additional mini reading / todo list:

  • CAKE: Compiler-Agent Co-Design for Frontier Kernel Evolution - seems interesting but the paper is kind of bare bones on first pass.
  • Want to be able to read the whole TPU pipeline. This primarily means setting up the TPU infrastructure but also a good set of blogs here, here, and here.
  • Need to come up with definitive proposal for project.
    • Limitations / survey of existing approaches.
      • Importantly, why non-agentic approaches cannot compete in JaxBench-regime.
      • Maybe walk through how each method does GQA?
    • Clear benchmarks to test.
    • Be able to defend choice of TPU.

As a bit of a productivity aside, I have noticed that I am wasting a lot of my prime deep work bandwidth in the morning on the transfer / organization of notes into this forest during reading. Additionally, my post-lunch block is very ineffective. I had the genius idea of eliminating the cognitive overhead of organizing and transferring notes by deferring until the afternoon block. In the morning block, I will just dump everything I might care about later. Then, in the later block, I will do the menial, low cognitive demand task of choosing what stays and where it goes. This can also serve as a form of review.

Furthermore, I have officially setup a technology free desk to take this to the extreme. This will hopefully serve as my paper reading station or when I am rich enough to get physical textbooks. I have an accompanying notebook to write down any questions I need to ask Claude after the pomo block ends, or write down relevant notes to transfer later. I have consistently avoided doing this because I believe quickly asking Claude is a necessity. However, I hope to find that writing down and thinking about questions independently first will not be that bad and may even be useful.

Until next week!
