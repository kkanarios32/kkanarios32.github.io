Kellen Kanarios [index]

    I am a PhD student at the University of Michigan as part of the Electrical and Computer Engineering program, where my work \(\in \) {programming languages} \(\cap \) {computer architecture} \(\cap \) {LLMs} \(\cap \) {reinforcement learning}. I am co-advised by Xinyu Wang and Satish Narayanasamy. Currently, I am interested in automated kernel generation across accelerators backends. In particular, neurosymbolic approaches to preserve guarantees and limit costs. With this, I am looking into {kernel DSLs, MLIR, formal synthesis, LLM "vibe compilers"}. I am also recreationally interested in Unix (see my dotfiles) and in a past life I did research in reinforcement learning algorithms.

    I earned my BS in Mathematics and Computer Science from the University of Michigan, graduating with honors and high honors, followed by an MS in Electrical and Computer Engineering. During this time, I was fortunate enough to be advised by Lei Ying. My resume is available here.

    This is my forestshoutout to Jon Sterling who is remarkably helpful. This website functions both as a personal knowledge management system, where I store my notes in notebooks, some accompanying solutions in the solution manuals, and any external resources I have made in /dev/null.
