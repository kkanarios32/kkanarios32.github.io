Kellen Kanarios [index]

    I am a PhD student at the University of Michigan as part of the Electrical and Computer Engineering program, where my work \(\in \) {programming languages} \(\cap \) {computer architecture} \(\cap \) {LLMs} \(\cap \) {reinforcement learning}. I am advised by Prof. Xinyu Wang and Prof. Satish Narayanasamy. Currently, I am interested in automated kernel generation across accelerators. In particular, neurosymbolic approaches to preserve guarantees and limit costs. With this, I am looking into {kernel DSLs, MLIR, formal synthesis, LLM "vibe compilers"}. I am also recreationally interested in Unix (see my dotfiles) and in a past life I did research in reinforcement learning algorithms.

    Previously, I obtained a BS from the University of Michigan in Mathematics and Computer Science, where I graduated with honors and high honors respectively. I also obtained my MS in Electrical and Computer Engineering from University of Michigan. During both my undergrad and masters, I worked with Lei Ying as part of the SURE Program, a TA for EECS 602, a GSRA, and a GSI for ECE 567. My full resume is available here.

    If you are a student at University of Michigan and are interested in research, I am advising a few undergraduates. For potential projects, see my Idea Purgatory. If you want to take one on, schedule a meeting!
