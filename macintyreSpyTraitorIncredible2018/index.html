      Review: Candide [4SOC]

      • Kellen Kanarios

      I did very little research prior to reading this book and was not at all aware of what I was getting into. It turns out this book is quite the controversial piece of philosophy. For what was supposed to be a light read, I ended up deep in the weeds on the meaning of life and flaws of humanity. The book is basically a stance against the idea of blind optimisim and the notion that everything is as it should be. More importantly, even if this were the case (in El Dorado) the human condition would not allow us to simply sit idle in such a place. However, I actually found the conclusion highly inspiring.       For what it is, I think it is a 7/10.

      Review: Elon Musk [2I8C]

      • July 28, 2026
      • Kellen Kanarios

      If nothing else, this book made me realize I need to work harder. I am not sure that daddy issues is a sufficient explanation for some of Musk's highly erratic actions. However, it is indisputable the drive and work ethic he both has and demands from his companies. I often wonder whether a relentless tyrant like Musk could maximize my potential, but it is very apparent here that it would come at a severe cost. Getting back to the book, I think it felt a little propaganda-y. I find it hard to believe Elon is a pure intentioned savior of humanity. Though, I do not doubt he likes to seem himself that way.

      Twitter part was too long, 5/10.
