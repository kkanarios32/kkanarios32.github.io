I have spent SO much time on my computer this week. My eyes are in a perpetual state of bloodshotIf somehow someone reading this has good eyedrops or some other remedy PLEASE reach out. This ultrawide monitor seemed like a good idea until one actually spends ~10hrs a day staring at it.

An eternity was spent on this forest. A good portion of this has been toward finally sitting down and getting a grip on TPUs via How to scale your model. I have actually made decent progress on the exercises and the corresponding notebook.

Unfortunately, the accompanying beautiful animations found in ReduceScatter and Systolic array were a less productive but equally tracked usage of my time.

The tokamax time is a bit of a hint to what I am cooking up. Due to the likely scenario no one is reading this, there will be an accompanying blog I am also working on Bringing Speed-of-Light FlexAttention to TPU. If {people working on this} \(\cap \) {people reading this} \(\neq \emptyset \). Then email me and we can combine forces!

I mean these numbers speak for themselves. \(50\)hrs actively on a computer are enough to make the terminators' eye blood vessels detonate. Luckily only my eyes and not my brain are rotting.
