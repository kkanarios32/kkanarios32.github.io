    Goals for Next Week

    • August 9, 2026
    • Kellen Kanarios

    My main goal over the next few months is becoming a TPU sensei. To this end, I am working on a blog traversing deep into the weeds of the TPU ecosystem. I am also working through the How to scale your model with solutions here and notebook here. Working on some Pallas kernels to hopefully be released some time in the next few months.

    A bit of reflection. I officially think it is time I focus and specialize. Throughout the past few years, I have had far too diverse interests and trying to learn everything everywhere all at once (never actually seen this movie :) ). While I have had a ton of fun learning computer architecture (here and here), operating systems (here), reinforcement learning, etc. it is simply not optimal to spread myself this thin and continues to lead to burnout. I am big on the idea that it is important to conduct some of Cal Newport's deliberate practice. Previously, I was trying to concurrently switch between different things each day, but I am now convinced I need to minimize cognitive overhead around decision making / maintenance / conducting said practice. From now on, I will pick one thing (scaling book at the time of this), do exercises, make anki cards, then switch after I feel VERY comfortable with the material and continue to maintain this understanding through anki.

    To do so, I will now have two deep work blocks each day of roughly two hours. The first will be this deliberate practice. Over time, we will see if this periodization approach will be more fruitful than trying to concurrently learn too much.
