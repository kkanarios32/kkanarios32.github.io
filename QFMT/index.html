I run. A lot.

Yeah I take creatine guysand you should too.. Outside of that, I am a bit of a bum. I reduced my stretch routine to a mere 23min and that actually has allowed me to pick my consistency back up. Leetcode is a more of an active choice than laziness. I have grown bearish on the value of the exercise and if I want to throw my name in the DSA rat race. I think my time is better spent on anything TPU or GPU related.

In addition to my general computer science cards, I have begun to make a deck for the scaling book. This is the first time I have been actively reviewing cards as I progress through a book, and it makes a world of a difference. Will definitely be doing this for the next book.
